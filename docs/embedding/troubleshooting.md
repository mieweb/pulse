# Troubleshooting

Find the code from the `PulseError` your call rejected with (`e.code`), from a `failed` result
(`result.error.code`), or from an upload's error (`draft.upload.error.code`). Every error carries a
`callId`; search PulseKit's `[PulseKit]` log lines for it ([dev-loop.md](dev-loop.md#logs)).
[contract.md](contract.md#errors) lists every code.

## Pulse doesn't open

| Code or symptom                                | Cause                                                                                                                                                                              | What to do                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `unavailable`                                  | The page has no Pulse plugin: a desktop browser, a web build, or the plugin isn't installed or synced.                                                                             | Show your fallback (a link or QR code). In the app, check that the plugin is in `package.json` and that `npx cap sync ios` or `cordova prepare ios` ran after installing it. |
| `isAvailable()` is false on an iPhone          | The app links a PulseKit without its native class: Xcode's console shows "PulseKit isn't linked into this app". `pulsekit-v0.1.0-rc.4` was built that way.                         | Use a later release, or a local build ([dev-loop.md](dev-loop.md)).                                                                                                          |
| `unsupported`                                  | `getInfo().supported` is false: iOS before 16.4, an iPad, a Mac, or Android.                                                                                                       | Show your fallback. `getInfo().unsupportedReason` says which: `os`, `ipad` or `device`.                                                                                      |
| `no_account`                                   | `open` or `pendingResults` before `setAccount()`, or after `setAccount(null)`.                                                                                                     | Call `setAccount()` after `configure()` on every page load ([accounts.md](accounts.md)).                                                                                     |
| `restart_required`                             | `setAccount()` for a different person after Pulse ran in this app session.                                                                                                         | Ask the person to restart the app; reads already answer for the new account ([accounts.md](accounts.md#switch-to-another-person)).                                           |
| `contract.unsupported`                         | The client and PulseKit have different contract majors.                                                                                                                            | Install the same exact version of `@mieweb/pulse-embed` and the plugin.                                                                                                      |
| `method.unsupported`                           | This PulseKit doesn't list the method in `getInfo().methods`, for example `listDrafts` on a minor-0 PulseKit.                                                                      | Check `methods.includes(name)` before calling, and hide the feature when it's missing.                                                                                       |
| `busy`                                         | A flow is already open with another `requestId`, or your app is presenting a screen (an alert, a sheet).                                                                           | Wait for the open flow's result, or dismiss your own screen first. To join the open flow instead, call `open` with its `requestId`.                                          |
| `invalid_options`                              | A field is missing or has the wrong type: often `context` without `label`, a `contextKey` with characters outside `[a-z0-9._:-]`, or a `context` over 8 KB.                        | Read `e.message`; it names the field. Build `key` with `contextKey(kind, id)`.                                                                                               |
| `host_misconfigured`                           | Your app's `Info.plist` lacks `NSCameraUsageDescription` or `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` (`e.details.missing` lists them), or the plugin had no view controller to present from. | Add the keys ([Info.plist keys](README.md#2-add-the-infoplist-keys)) and rebuild. Opening Pulse before your app's first screen is up can also cause it.                      |
| Save to Photos is missing on Export            | `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` isn't in your `Info.plist`, or `policy.allowSaveToPhotos` is false.                                                                            | Add the key, or remove the policy.                                                                                                                                           |
| A second `flowResult` for the same `requestId` | PulseKit ended the flow itself (your `close()` got no answer in 5 s, or your app dismissed Pulse), then Pulse's own result arrived.                                                | Expected. Act on the latest result, and make handling a `requestId` twice harmless.                                                                                          |
| No result after a page reload                  | The `open` promise belonged to the old page.                                                                                                                                       | Call `pendingResults()` on every page load and handle what it returns.                                                                                                       |

## The flow ended `failed`

| Code                   | Cause                                                                                                  | What to do                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `not_found`            | `edit` or `export` named a draft that isn't on this phone (deleted, or sent and retired).              | Re-read the list with `listDrafts()`.                                                                                                                                |
| `draft.locked`         | `edit` or `export` named a draft that is sending.                                                      | Wait for the upload, or `cancelUpload(id)` first if it is queued or uploading.                                                                                       |
| `storage.full`         | The phone is out of space. Pulse said so and kept the videos.                                          | Ask the person to free up space, then open Pulse again.                                                                                                              |
| `storage.damaged`      | Pulse couldn't open or update its library on this phone.                                               | Report it with the `callId` and `logFile()`. `error.details.reset: true` means the person tapped Reset; this version deletes nothing, so the library is still there. |
| `storage.newer_schema` | A newer PulseKit wrote this library, and your app now runs an older one.                               | Ask the person to update the app. Until then, lists, Send and Delete still work.                                                                                     |
| `export.failed`        | After "Use this video", Pulse couldn't copy the video out for your app. The draft is kept (`draftId`). | Open it again with `exportDraft(draftId)`; if it fails again, report it with the log.                                                                                |
| `runtime.error`        | A JavaScript error inside Pulse. Your app keeps running.                                               | Open Pulse again; the next `open` starts a fresh runtime. Report it with `logFile()` and the crash's source map ([releasing.md](releasing.md#symbolicate-a-crash)).  |

## A drafts call rejected

| Code or symptom                             | Cause                                                                                                                    | What to do                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `draft.locked`                              | The draft is preparing, uploading or processing, or open in the current flow. `reason: 'clock_bound'`: a confirmed Send. | Offer only what the [state allows](uploads.md#what-your-list-shows); a clock-bound draft can't be moved.         |
| `not_found`                                 | The draft is gone: deleted, or sent and retired into a receipt.                                                          | Re-read the list.                                                                                                |
| `timeout`                                   | Pulse didn't answer in 30 s, its start included. The change may still have happened.                                     | Re-read the draft before you retry. `createPulseClient({ timeoutMs })` gives slow phones longer.                 |
| `host_misconfigured`                        | `upload(id)` with `uploadMode: 'file'`.                                                                                  | Configure `uploadMode: 'pulse'` and set your host services ([uploads.md](uploads.md)).                           |
| `upload.too_large`                          | `upload(id)` on a video over its upload type's `maxBytes`.                                                               | Offer Edit, so the person can trim it.                                                                           |
| `storage.newer_schema`                      | A rename or a Move to… in a library a newer PulseKit wrote.                                                              | Ask the person to update the app.                                                                                |
| A "Preparing…" screen flashes over your app | A drafts write, or `resumeOutbox()`, started Pulse in a build without the `headless` capability.                         | Expected in such a build. Don't call `resumeOutbox()` on launch there ([uploads.md](uploads.md#paused-uploads)). |

## An upload doesn't finish

| State or code                                  | Cause                                                                                                                              | What to do                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stays `queued` or `uploading` after a relaunch | Pulse isn't running in this app session, and the build has no `headless` capability.                                               | Show "Paused · Tap to resume" and call `resumeOutbox()` from the tap ([uploads.md](uploads.md#paused-uploads)).                                                     |
| `waiting_network`                              | Offline, or `policy.wifiOnlyUploads` and the phone is on cellular.                                                                 | Nothing; it goes on when the network comes back.                                                                                                                    |
| `needs_credentials` with `host.unavailable`    | Your page didn't answer a host request within 20 s, or threw without a code.                                                       | Make sure `setHostServices()` runs on every page load and its handlers settle; then `resumeOutbox()`.                                                               |
| `needs_credentials` with `no_account`          | Nobody was signed in, or another person is.                                                                                        | It goes on after `setAccount()` for the same person.                                                                                                                |
| `needs_credentials` with `credentials.expired` | The token expired and `refreshUploadToken` failed.                                                                                 | Check your token route; then `resumeOutbox()`.                                                                                                                      |
| `failed` with `destination.gone`               | Your server said the place is gone or the person lost access.                                                                      | Offer Move to… or Delete.                                                                                                                                           |
| `failed` with `upload.refused`                 | Your server or PulseVault refused the upload for good.                                                                             | Show the reason; offer Retry (`upload(id)`), Move to…, Edit or Delete.                                                                                              |
| `failed` with `upload.too_large`               | The video is over your server's limit.                                                                                             | Offer Edit to trim it.                                                                                                                                              |
| `failed` with `host_misconfigured`             | Your `getUploadTarget` returned a target whose `clientKey` isn't the one Pulse asked for.                                          | Echo the request's `clientKey`.                                                                                                                                     |
| Stays `delivering`                             | The bytes are on the server, and it hasn't recorded an outcome yet.                                                                | Check your server's processing. Pulse asks again while it runs, and retires the draft once the outcome is there.                                                    |
| Finishes only after the person opens the app   | The person force-quit the app (iOS stops its background uploads), or your `AppDelegate` doesn't forward background session events. | Forward them ([uploads.md](uploads.md#forward-background-upload-events)). After a force-quit, the upload resumes from the server's offset the next time Pulse runs. |
